======================================================
  RK PLAYER - Modern Media Player for Windows (v1.0.0)
======================================================
Thank you for downloading RK Player!

Quick Start:
1. Double-click RK-Player-Setup.exe to begin installation.
2. Follow on-screen instructions.
3. Open RK Player and drag & drop any video or audio file.

Supported Formats:
MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WEBM, MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV, M4A, etc.

Official Website: https://rkplayer.com
Support & Bug Reports: support@rkplayer.com
